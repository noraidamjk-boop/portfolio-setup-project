# B2B SaaS Marketing Playbook

## Executive Summary

This playbook presents a practical Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for B2B SaaS marketing. It combines insights from ten industry experts covering demand generation, content marketing, positioning, AI marketing, SEO, personal branding, and growth strategies.

The objective is to help B2B SaaS companies build sustainable demand, strengthen brand authority, and generate qualified pipeline through consistent marketing execution.

---

## Objectives

- Build sustainable demand generation.
- Improve brand positioning.
- Create educational content that builds trust.
- Increase qualified leads.
- Continuously optimize marketing performance.

---

# Standard Operating Procedure
## Step 1 – Define Your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Objective
Identify the specific companies and decision-makers that will benefit the most from your product.

### Actions

- Define the target industry.
- Define company size.
- Identify key decision-makers.
- List common business challenges.
- Identify buying triggers.

### Deliverables

- ICP Document
- Buyer Persona

### Success Metrics

- Marketing team uses one consistent ICP.
- Sales and marketing target the same audience.

### Source

## Step 2 – Research Customer Problems

### Objective

Understand real customer challenges before creating marketing campaigns.

### Actions

- Interview existing customers.
- Review customer feedback.
- Analyze sales objections.
- Review support tickets.
- Identify recurring questions.

### Deliverables

- Pain Point List
- Customer Language Document

### Success Metrics

- Marketing messages clearly address customer pain points.
- Sales objections decrease over time.

### Source

Rand Fishkin

Platform: LinkedIn / YouTube

Topic:

## Step 3 – Create Clear Positioning

### Objective

Develop a positioning statement that explains who the company helps, what problem it solves, and why it is different.

### Actions

- Write a one-sentence positioning statement.
- Avoid generic marketing language.
- Focus on measurable business outcomes.
- Validate positioning with customers.

### Deliverables

- Positioning Statement
- Messaging Guide

### Success Metrics

- Consistent messaging across website, sales, and social media.
